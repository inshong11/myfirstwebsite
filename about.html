MY STORY

More Than Just One Name

My name is Ian Vincent A. Encio. To my close friends, I'm Aya. To those who know me through the stage, performance, and pageantry, I'm Veena Cava. Different names, different spaces, but all reflections of the same person: someone who believes in authenticity, self-expression, and growth.

I am a 20-year-old nursing student from Koronadal City, South Cotabato, currently pursuing my dreams while navigating the beautiful complexities of life. My journey has never been limited to a single path. While I chose nursing because of my desire to care for others, I have also found myself drawn to the arts, performance, and storytelling.

As a nursing student, I am learning the value of compassion, empathy, and service. Every clinical experience reminds me that caring for others goes beyond treatment. It is about seeing people for who they are and making them feel heard, valued, and understood. These lessons continue to shape the healthcare professional I aspire to become.

Outside the classroom and hospital, I express myself through creativity. I enjoy journaling, digital drawing, watercolor painting, and exploring different artistic outlets. Art has always been a space where I can freely express my thoughts, emotions, and imagination. It allows me to tell stories that words alone sometimes cannot capture.

One of the most transformative parts of my journey has been pageantry and performance. Through these experiences, I discovered confidence not as the absence of fear, but as the courage to embrace who I truly am. As Veena Cava, I celebrate creativity, artistry, and self-expression. Through every performance and every stage I step onto, I am reminded that authenticity is one of the greatest gifts we can give ourselves and others.

When I'm not studying or creating, you'll probably find me listening to Linger by The Cranberries, rewatching Coraline, singing songs from Hamilton, enjoying cheese and crackers, or spending time with the people who matter most to me. I believe that life's smallest joys are often the most meaningful.

At my core, I am someone who is still growing, still learning, and still becoming. My story is far from finished, but every experience, challenge, and triumph has shaped me into the person I am today. Whether you know me as Ian, Aya, or Veena Cava, I hope this space offers a glimpse into the passions, dreams, and values that continue to guide my journey.
